# Asset Needed

The procedural factory pipeline is active and intentionally keeps all story and interaction systems independent from art assets. To reach the requested premium cinematic quality, the following replacement assets are needed.

## 1. Couple

- Format: one optimized GLB, preferably Draco-compressed.
- Style: stylized cinematic, young adult couple, seated embrace; woman leaning on man; man holding her and pointing toward the sky.
- Requirements: clean silhouette from front/left/right/back, separate materials for skin, hair, clothing, and shoes; no copyrighted character/IP resemblance.
- License: CC0, CC-BY with attribution recorded in this repository, or a purchased commercial web-use license.

## 2. Hero Tree

- Format: GLB with separate trunk, branches, roots, and foliage meshes.
- Style: ancient stylized tree with broad organic canopy, muted sage leaves and small dusty-pink blossom accents.
- Requirements: visible roots, no billboard-only foliage, optimized for mobile web.
- License: CC0, CC-BY with attribution, or purchased commercial web-use license.

## 3. Floating Garden Island

- Format: GLB with separate garden surface, soil edge, rocky underside, and reusable detail meshes.
- Style: asymmetrical magical garden, compatible with the same stylized material language as the tree/couple.
- License: CC0, CC-BY with attribution, or purchased commercial web-use license.

## Integration

Once supplied, these assets should replace only the corresponding factory output in `assets/factories.js`. Password, camera controls, seven memory lanterns, modal, progress, music, and ending logic should not require changes.
